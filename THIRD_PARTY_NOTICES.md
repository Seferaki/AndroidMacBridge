# Third-party notices

## Bundled word lists

`android/app/src/main/assets/words/ru.txt` and `en.txt` are derived from the 2018 Russian and English FrequencyWords lists by Hermit Dave. The lists were filtered to alphabetic entries and limited to 20,000 entries per language. The upstream corpus is based on OpenSubtitles. The included `LICENSE-FrequencyWords.txt` contains its MIT license; `NOTICE.txt` records provenance.

## Build dependencies

Android and macOS dependencies are downloaded by their respective build tools from the upstream repositories declared in the project files. Those dependencies remain subject to their own licenses and notices.
