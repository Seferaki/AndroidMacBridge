The word lists are derived from the 2018 Russian and English FrequencyWords lists:
https://github.com/hermitdave/FrequencyWords (copyright 2016 Hermit Dave).
Only alphabetic entries are retained, in frequency order, up to 20,000 entries per language.
See LICENSE-FrequencyWords.txt for the list license. The underlying corpus is OpenSubtitles:
https://opus.nlpl.eu/OpenSubtitles.php
